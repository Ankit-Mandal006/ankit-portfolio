# Space Shooter

> *Outmaneuver the swarm. Obliterate the boss.*

## Overview

An exciting arcade space shooter built around high-intensity wave survival and multi-stage boss encounters. Navigate through escalating swarms of hostile ships, react quickly to fast-moving chargers, and overcome massive titans in precision-tuned retro space combat.

---

## Key Features

* **Wave-Based Combat:** Survive continuous, escalating waves of enemy ships with varied movement and attack patterns.
* **Boss Fights:** Engage in multi-phase battles against heavy bosses equipped with dedicated health bars.
* **Polished Mechanics:** Includes full pause/resume state management and screen-flash damage effects for clear tactical combat feedback.
* **Arcade Visual FX:** Custom sprite animations paired with dynamic explosion particle systems.

---

## AI-Accelerated Development

Utilized AI-assisted workflows during production to eliminate boilerplate development. This allowed the team to allocate maximum focus toward refining game mechanics, combat feel, visual feedback, and overall user experience.
