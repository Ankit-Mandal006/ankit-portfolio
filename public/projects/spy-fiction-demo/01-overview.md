# Spy-Fiction

> *Infiltrate the unseen. Investigate the truth. Survive the trap.*

## Overview

**Spy-Fiction** is a stealth-driven experience where you play as Agent 67, an elite operative deployed to infiltrate a heavily guarded hostile facility. 

Your mission is to avoid detection, gather classified intelligence, and uncover fragments of a hidden plot. However, as you penetrate deeper into the compound, a chilling realization unfolds—your presence wasn't an unexpected breach, but a calculated invitation.

---

## Key Features

* **Tactical Stealth Mechanics:** Infiltrate hostile zones by maintaining low visibility and avoiding enemy detection lines.
* **Layered Animator System:** Dynamic state blending transitions seamlessly between standard locomotion and tactical aim-walking layers.
* **Intel & Story Extraction:** Collect classified documents and engage in interactive dialogues to unravel the narrative piece by piece.

---

## Controls

| Action | Input | Logic / Behavior |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Move** | `W` `A` `S` `D` | Full speed movement (Base Layer) |
| **Aim** | `Right Click` | Transitions Animator to Aim Walk layer |
| **Shoot** | `Left Click` | Discharges weapon *(Only active while holding Aim)* |
| **Interact** | `E` | Collect Documents / Advance Dialogue |
