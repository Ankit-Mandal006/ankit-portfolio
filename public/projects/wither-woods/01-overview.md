# The Living Forest

> *Follow the light. Listen to the storm. Escape the dream.*

## Overview

A surreal survival-horror experience where players must escape an ancient, soul-draining forest using magnetic tracking and visual/auditory environmental cues.

An ordinary night decays into a nightmare as you awaken in an eerie, sentient woodland. The forest protects itself by feeding on the souls of those trapped within, absorbing them into its roots for eternity. Whispers, riddles, and cryptic spirits echo through the dark—offering guidance or luring you deeper into the trap. To survive, you must decipher environmental clues, brave shifting realities, and reach the portal before the forest consumes you completely.

---

## Survival Navigation Rules

* **Magnetic Tracking:** Your compass always targets the strongest magnetic field. Rely on it as a navigational anchor when the environment shifts.
* **Portal Seeking:** Thunderbolts strike directly over the exit portal. Follow the light flashes and visual rumbles to locate your escape route.

---

## Key Elements

* **Living Forest:** Dynamic, ever-changing pathways that react directly to your presence.
* **Shadowed Streets:** Pitch-black remnants of a forgotten world that amplify isolation and unease.
* **Dreamlike Reality:** Visual distortions and shifting rules that blur the boundary between real and imagined.
