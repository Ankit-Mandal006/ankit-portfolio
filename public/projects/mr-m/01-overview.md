# Mr. M

> *The more you earn, the harder it gets.*

## Overview

**Mr. M** is a dynamic platformer and arena challenge game where progression directly ramps up the difficulty. As you collect badges, the world scales in complexity, shifting across vastly different environments and hazards—from top-down sewer pest arenas to treacherous volcanic runs, spiked canyon drops, and forest canopy platforming. 

---

## Key Features

* **Dynamic Badge Scaling:** Collecting badges continuously increases game difficulty, introducing harsher obstacles and faster hazards.
* **Multi-Genre Environments:** Experience diverse gameplay perspectives and levels, including top-down arena survival and classic 2D side-scrolling platforming.
* **High-Mobility Controls:** Master precise jumping and quick directional dashes to maneuver past crushing hammers, rolling boulders, and ambient hazards.

---

## Controls

| Input | Action | Behavior |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `W` `A` `S` `D` | **Movement** | Move character across 2D/Top-down space |
| `Space` | **Jump** | Vertical traversal |
| `Left Alt` | **Dash** | Evasive dash burst |
