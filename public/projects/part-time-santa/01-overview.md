# Save Christmas

> *Santa’s sick. Deliver joy. Sleigh the night.*

## Overview

Santa has fallen ill right on Christmas Eve, putting the holiday in jeopardy! Chosen directly by Santa himself, you must step in, carry the night, and bring presents to every home. Quick reflexes and accurate judgment are critical—sort the naughty from the nice, avoid misfires, and save Christmas before dawn.

---

## Scoring & Gameplay Rules

* **Proper Deliveries:** Shoot gifts at **nice kids** (`+4 Points`).
* **Laying Coal:** Shoot coal at **naughty kids** (`+4 Points`).
* **Misfires:** Shooting the wrong item at the wrong kid (`-1 Point`).

---

## Controls

| Action | Input | Behavior |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Movement** | `W` `A` `S` `D` | 8-Directional movement |
| **Shoot** | `Left Click` | Fire selected projectile |
| **Autoshoot** | `Hold Left Click` | Continuous rapid fire |
| **Dash** | `Right Click` | Quick tactical dash |
| **Switch Ammo** | `Mouse Scroll` | Toggle between Gifts and Coal |
